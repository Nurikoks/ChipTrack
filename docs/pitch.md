# 5-minute pitch outline

1. **Problem (30 s).** Reviewing cashier video by hand is slow. The bank needs *where to look*, not a verdict: timecode, frame, and the rule that may be affected.
2. **What we found in the data (45 s).** 40 staged clips with no types given. We identified 12 visible violation types (cash or bag left on the desk, unlocked PC, safe left open, key in lock, cash left as the next client approaches, outsider in the cash zone, door left open, counting without the client, collector without documents, personal items, drinking, cash handed to a client at the desk). Most types are backed by frames contrasted with normal clips; the cash-handover type rests on one labelled clip.
3. **Approach (75 s).** Fully local on a 4 GB laptop GPU. RT-DETRv2 finds and tracks people. Qwen3-VL-2B (Apache-2.0) grounds the desk, safe, laptop and door **per video**, so there are no fixed pixel zones and it is ready for a new camera angle. Events are relations between people and those anchors (left the workplace, entered at the door, changeover). Each event triggers constrained yes/no visual checks on crops. Adding a type means adding one question, one trigger and one rule.
4. **Normative grounding (30 s).** 20 clauses from 3 RK acts in force (NBRK No. 231 cash rules, NBRK No. 14 premises, ARDFM No. 53 information security), each verified word for word on adilet.zan.kz. Weak bases are labelled "indirect" or "interpretation"; nothing is invented.
5. **Demo (60 s).** Upload in the UI → "Обнаружены признаки возможных нарушений" → type, mm:ss timecode, frames, rule and link. Show the same thing through curl and the JSON.
6. **Results (30 s).** Training clips, in-sample (thresholds set on the same clips): 18 of 21 violation clips flagged (recall 0.86); 9 of 19 normal clips also flagged (precision 0.67). We chose recall: a false alarm costs a reviewer a glance, a missed violation is never seen. The labelled type is among the flagged types for 18 of 22 events; median timecode error 1.9 s; ~26 s per clip on an RTX 4060 (the previous version took 27 s on the 4 GB RTX 3050). 4 parallel uploads done in 97.5 s in the local API test.
7. **Limitations and growth (30 s).** Key in lock and door state are below what the camera resolves at this distance, and 2 training events remain unexplained. Next: higher-resolution crops or a second camera on the safe, a small fine-tuned verifier once more labelled clips exist, active learning from reviewer feedback in the UI.
